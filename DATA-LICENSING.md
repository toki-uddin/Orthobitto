# Dictionary data licensing and provenance

Orthobitto must not silently package third-party dictionary data without checking the dataset's current license and redistribution terms.

Recommended validation sources:
- AI4Bharat Indic Glossaries reports 102,855 English-Bengali glossary records. The repository itself is MIT-licensed, while the collected datasets have their own provenance/terms and must be reviewed before redistribution.
- Open English WordNet is CC-BY 4.0 and is suitable as an English lexical-validation source.
- Any third-party English→Bengali dataset must be imported only after its redistribution terms are cleared for the intended Orthobitto distribution.
